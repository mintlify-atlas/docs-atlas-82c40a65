Welcome to the most exciting topic in AI today: **Agents**!

This free course takes you on a journey **from beginner to expert** in understanding, using, and building AI agents.

This first unit will help you get started:

- Discover the **course syllabus**
- **Choose the path** you'll take — self-audit or certification
- **Learn about the certification process**
- Get to know the team behind the course
- Create your **Hugging Face account**
- **Sign up to our Discord server** and meet your classmates

## What to expect from this course

In this course, you will:

- Study AI agents in **theory, design, and practice**
- Learn to **use established AI agent libraries** such as [smolagents](https://huggingface.co/docs/smolagents/en/index), [LlamaIndex](https://www.llamaindex.ai/), and [LangGraph](https://langchain-ai.github.io/langgraph/)
- **Share your agents** on the Hugging Face Hub and explore agents created by the community
- Participate in challenges where you **evaluate your agents against other students'**
- **Earn a certificate of completion** by completing assignments

By the end of this course, you'll understand **how agents work and how to build your own agents** using the latest libraries and tools.

<Note>
  Don't forget to [sign up to the course](https://bit.ly/hf-learn-agents)! We collect your email address to send you links when each unit is published and to keep you informed about challenges and updates.
</Note>

## What does the course look like

The course is composed of:

- **Foundational units** — where you learn agent concepts in theory
- **Hands-on sections** — where you'll use established AI agent libraries in pre-configured Hugging Face Spaces
- **Use case assignments** — where you apply what you've learned to solve a real-world problem of your choosing
- **The Challenge** — where your agent competes against other agents, with results tracked on [a leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/agents-course/Students_leaderboard)

<Tip>
  This course is a living project that evolves with your feedback and contributions. Feel free to [open issues and PRs on GitHub](https://github.com/huggingface/agents-course) and join discussions on Discord.
</Tip>

After completing the course, you can also submit feedback [using this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe9VaONn0eglax0uTwi29rIn4tM7H2sYmmybmG5jJNlE5v0xA/viewform?usp=dialog).

## Syllabus

Here is the general syllabus for the course. A more detailed list of topics will be released with each unit.

| Chapter | Topic | Description |
| :------ | :---- | :---------- |
| 0 | Onboarding | Set you up with the tools and platforms you will use. |
| 1 | Agent fundamentals | Explain tools, thoughts, actions, observations, and their formats. Explain LLMs, messages, special tokens, and chat templates. Show a simple use case using Python functions as tools. |
| 2 | Frameworks | Understand how the fundamentals are implemented in popular libraries: smolagents, LangGraph, LlamaIndex. |
| 3 | Use cases | Build real-life use cases (open to PRs from experienced agent builders). |
| 4 | Final assignment | Build an agent for a selected benchmark and prove your understanding on the student leaderboard. |

In addition to the main syllabus, there are three bonus units:

## Prerequisites

To follow this course, you should have:

- Basic knowledge of Python
- Basic knowledge of LLMs (Unit 1 includes a recap section)

## What tools do you need

You only need two things:

- A **computer** with an internet connection
- A **Hugging Face account** — to push and load models, agents, and create Spaces. If you don't have one yet, [create one here](https://hf.co/join) (it's free)

## Certification process

<img
  src="https://huggingface.co/datasets/agents-course/course-images/resolve/main/en/unit0/three-paths.jpg"
  alt="Three course paths: audit, fundamentals certificate, completion certificate"
/>

You can follow this course in audit mode or complete activities to earn one of two certificates. The certification process is **completely free** with **no deadline**.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Fundamentals certificate" icon="certificate">
    Complete Unit 1. Intended for students who want to get up to date with the latest trends in agents.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Certificate of completion" icon="graduation-cap">
    Complete Unit 1, one use case assignment, and the final challenge.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  If you audit the course, you can still participate in challenges and complete assignments — you just don't need to notify us.
</Note>

## How to get the most out of this course

1. **Join study groups on Discord** — studying in groups is always easier. [Join our Discord server](https://discord.gg/UrrTSsSyjb) and verify your Hugging Face account.
2. **Do the quizzes and assignments** — the best way to learn is through hands-on practice and self-assessment.
3. **Define a schedule** — use the recommended pace above or create your own.
