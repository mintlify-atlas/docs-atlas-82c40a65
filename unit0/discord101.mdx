This guide helps you get started with Discord, the free chat platform used by the Hugging Face community. The [Hugging Face Community Discord server](https://discord.gg/UrrTSsSyjb) has over 100,000 members and is a great place to connect, ask questions, and share your work.

## The agents course on Discord

The HF Community server hosts discussions across many areas of machine learning, including paper discussions, events, and library-specific support channels.

After [signing up](http://hf.co/join/discord), introduce yourself in the `#introduce-yourself` channel.

Four channels are dedicated to the Agents Course:

## How to join the server

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the invite link">
    Open [discord.gg/UrrTSsSyjb](https://discord.gg/UrrTSsSyjb).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or create an account">
    Sign in with your existing Discord account, or create a new one if you don't have one yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the verification">
    Confirm that you are not an AI agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your profile">
    Choose your nickname and avatar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join the server">
    Click **Join Server**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  For a more detailed walkthrough, see Discord's official guide on [how to join a server](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034842871-How-do-I-join-a-Server#h_01FSJF9GT2QJMS2PRAW36WNBS8).
</Note>

## Tips for using Discord effectively

- **Voice channels** are available, though text chat is more commonly used.
- **Format code with markdown** — wrap code in backticks for inline snippets or triple backticks for blocks. This makes it much easier for others to read your questions.
- **Open threads for long conversations** — this keeps the main channel tidy and makes discussions easier to follow.

If you have any questions, ask in the `#agents-course-questions` channel.
