If you scored **30% or above**, you are eligible to claim your official Certificate of Completion.

## How to claim your certificate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the certificate page">
    Go to the [certificate page](https://huggingface.co/spaces/agents-course/Unit4-Final-Certificate).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Hugging Face">
    Use the sign-in button on the page to authenticate with your Hugging Face account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your full name">
    Type your full name as you would like it to appear on your certificate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your certificate">
    Click **"Get My Certificate"** to verify your score and download the certificate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

![Certificate congratulations screen](https://huggingface.co/datasets/agents-course/course-images/resolve/main/en/unit4/congrats.png)

## Share your achievement

Once you have your certificate, consider sharing it with your network:

- Add it to your **LinkedIn profile**
- Share it on **X**, **Bluesky**, or other social platforms

Tag [@huggingface](https://huggingface.co/huggingface) when you share — the team would love to celebrate with you.

<Tip>
  If you run into any issues during submission, open a discussion on the [certification community tab](https://huggingface.co/spaces/agents-course/Unit4-Final-Certificate/discussions).
</Tip>
