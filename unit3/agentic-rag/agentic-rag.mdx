## What is RAG?

LLMs are trained on enormous bodies of data to learn general knowledge. However, a model's world knowledge may not always be relevant or up to date. **RAG solves this problem by finding and retrieving relevant information from your data and forwarding it to the LLM.**

![RAG diagram](https://huggingface.co/datasets/agents-course/course-images/resolve/main/en/unit2/llama-index/rag.png)

## Why agentic RAG?

Now think about how Alfred works:

1. You've asked Alfred to help plan a gala.
2. Alfred needs to find the latest news and weather information.
3. Alfred needs to structure and search the guest information.

Just as Alfred needs to search through your household information to be helpful, any agent needs a way to find and understand relevant data. **Agentic RAG is a powerful way to use agents to answer questions about your data.** You can pass various tools to Alfred to help him answer questions. Instead of answering questions on top of documents automatically, Alfred can decide to use any other tool or flow to answer the question.

![Agentic RAG diagram](https://huggingface.co/datasets/agents-course/course-images/resolve/main/en/unit2/llama-index/agentic-rag.png)
