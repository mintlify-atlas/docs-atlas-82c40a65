## Welcome to the AI Agents Course

This free course takes you on a journey **from beginner to expert** in understanding, using, and building AI agents. You'll learn the fundamentals of agentic AI, master popular frameworks like smolagents, LangGraph, and LlamaIndex, and build real-world agent applications.

## What you'll learn

By completing this course, you will:

- **Understand AI Agents** — Learn what agents are, how they reason, and how they act using the Think → Act → Observe cycle
- **Master agent frameworks** — Get hands-on with smolagents, LangGraph, and LlamaIndex
- **Build real applications** — Create agents that search the web, analyze documents, retrieve data, and more
- **Deploy and share** — Publish your agents to Hugging Face Spaces
- **Earn certification** — Demonstrate your skills with official certificates

## Certification

<Info>
The certification process is **completely free** and has **no deadline**.
</Info>

There are two certificates available:

- **Certificate of Fundamentals** — Complete Unit 1 and pass the final quiz
- **Certificate of Completion** — Complete Unit 1, one use-case assignment, and the final challenge
